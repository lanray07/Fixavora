# Fixavora privacy policy — publisher review required

Draft date: 10 November 2026. This draft is not an assertion of legal compliance. Publisher legal identity, support contact, applicable jurisdictions and a publicly accessible policy URL must be supplied before release.

Fixavora stores home records, user-entered contact details, financial records and preferences locally in Android app-private storage. No account or precise location is required. Fixavora does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and does not silently upload home records or documents.

If you attach a file, the app stores a read-only reference provided by Android's document picker. Your chosen storage provider controls the original file, may use cloud storage and applies its own policies. If the file is moved or access is revoked, you may need to attach it again. Deleting an attachment link or Fixavora record does not delete that external file.

Microphone access is requested only when you tap to speak. The installed recognition engine may transmit audio to its provider. Fixavora does not retain microphone recordings. Spoken feedback is provided by the installed text-to-speech engine. You can type instead and turn off automatic spoken responses.

Amazon processes optional purchases. When configured, the receipt verification service receives an Amazon app-specific user ID and receipt ID to verify entitlement. These IDs are purchase data; no property records are needed for verification. The publisher must document that service's processor, region, retention and deletion arrangements before enabling it. Secrets are held on the server.

Exports and portable encrypted backups are saved only to a destination you select. Anyone with an exported report can read it. Backups require the passphrase; the publisher cannot recover it. External documents are not included. You are responsible for protecting your exported copies.

Use Settings → Delete all home data to remove local records, history, attachment links and preferences. Removing the app also removes its private storage. External files, backups you created and Amazon purchase records remain with their respective providers. Core app data is excluded from Android automatic backup.

The app does not certify property safety or provide hazardous repair instructions. Schedules are general organisation tools and must be adapted to manufacturer instructions and local requirements.

Before publication, the publisher must supply contact details for privacy requests, assess regional obligations and age suitability, verify all SDK/provider disclosures, and update this policy if optional remote features are enabled.
